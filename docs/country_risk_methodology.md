# Country risk monitor: methodology and controls

## Purpose

The monitor connects sovereign conditions to portfolio decisions: country exposure,
illustrative limit utilisation, early-warning escalation and current public UKEF cover
context. It is an independent screening model, not UKEF's methodology, an OECD country
classification or a sovereign credit rating.

## Public data

The versioned snapshot in `data/country_risk_world_bank.csv` is generated from the World
Bank Indicators API. It contains annual observations for GDP growth, inflation, current
account balance, import-cover reserves, external debt and three Worldwide Governance
Indicators. The refresh script is `scripts/refresh_country_risk_data.py`.

`data/ukef_cover_policy_snapshot.csv` records the public cover indication for every country
in the fictional portfolio. It is generated by `scripts/refresh_ukef_cover_policy.py` and
includes the snapshot date and source URL. Cover indications can change and must be checked
at source before use.

## Transparent score

Each latest available indicator is mapped linearly to a 0–100 risk score between disclosed
"good" and "bad" thresholds. The weighted average uses:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| GDP growth | 14% |
| Inflation | 14% |
| Current account | 10% |
| Reserves in months of imports | 14% |
| External debt / GNI | 12% |
| Political stability | 14% |
| Government effectiveness | 11% |
| Rule of law | 11% |

Missing indicators are excluded and remaining weights are normalised. The dashboard shows
coverage so sparse data cannot appear equivalent to full evidence. Grades are descriptive:
Low (0–20), Moderate (20–40), Elevated (40–60), High (60–80), Very high (80–100).

## Early-warning thresholds

Warnings flag contraction, inflation above 15%, a current-account deficit below -5% of
GDP, reserves below three months of imports, external debt above 70% of GNI, and weak
governance readings. These are screening thresholds for review, not automatic credit
decisions.

## Governance and limitations

- Annual public indicators lag fast-moving political and market events.
- Latest readings may have different vintages; the briefing reports the oldest vintage.
- Country risk is not the same as buyer risk, transaction structure or project risk.
- The fictional country limits are not UKEF limits and must not be compared directly with
  public market risk appetite as if they were equivalent measures.
- Expert judgement, data-quality review, sanctions/compliance checks, climate and security
  analysis, OECD classification and transaction-level mitigants remain outside this score.
- Threshold, weight and source changes should be version controlled, reviewed and
  backtested against defaults, restructurings, claims or rating migration before production.
